Caution! There is a bug --- though it doesn't affect the ground state. 

To express \phi = |\phi| * exp(i\theta), we prepared NNs for |\phi| and \theta. However, \theta may not be single-valued, and when |\theta| becomes zero, the winding of \theta can cause an issue. This doesn't affect the ground state of the bosonic theory because \theta is zero anyway.
